FAQ

Questions

Is this investment advice?

No. Reports describe current market conditions for educational purposes. They contain no ratings, recommendations, price targets or trading instructions, and they aren't tailored to you. Talk to a licensed financial professional before making investment decisions.

How long does a report take?

Quick reports usually arrive in 10-20 minutes and Deep reports in 20-45 minutes. If our quality check flags something, a person reviews the report first; you'll get it within 24 hours or a full refund.

What's the difference between Quick and Deep?

Quick covers trend, momentum, key levels, news and fundamentals (or on-chain data for crypto). Deep adds a social sentiment analysis and a multi-round review of the evidence on both sides.

How current is the data?

Reports use data available at the moment they run. Crypto trades around the clock, so a crypto report shows the price at report time and uses completed daily candles (which close at 00:00 UTC) for indicators. Stock reports use the latest completed trading session.

Can AI get things wrong?

Yes. That's why every figure is checked against the underlying market data before delivery and why data gaps are flagged in the report. Always verify important figures with original sources.

What if something goes wrong?

If we can't produce a report that passes quality control, you're refunded in full automatically.
