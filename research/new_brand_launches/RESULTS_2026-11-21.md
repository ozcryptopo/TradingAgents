## Notes added after the run (descriptive, not part of the pre-registered test)

- 72% of debuts had a single review by day 60 (median 1, 90th percentile 3,
  99th percentile 13). Most "brands" in the data are tiny sellers.
- Debuts in the top 10% of their cohort by day-60 reviews succeeded 41.6% of
  the time, vs. 6.4% for the rest (base rate 9.6%): roughly a 4x lift from
  review count alone.
- Among the 1,302 debuts with 10+ reviews by day 60, `no_negatives` made no
  difference (60.7% vs. 62.1% success). The large gap in the H3 table above
  comes from volume, not from missing bad reviews.
- In that same group, success fell as single-day bursts rose: 68.3% (low
  burst third), 59.8% (middle), 55.1% (high). This is a candidate for a new,
  separately registered test on categories this run has not touched.
